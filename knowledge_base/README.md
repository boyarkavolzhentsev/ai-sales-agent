# Knowledge Base

Approved source material the AI Sales Agent may use to ground its replies.

**Rule:** only verified, approved information belongs here. If a question cannot be answered
from this knowledge base, the agent must escalate to the operator rather than invent an answer.

This directory currently contains no company data — only the domain structure.

## Knowledge domains

| # | Domain | Folder | What belongs here |
|---|--------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | Company | `company/` | Company overview, mission, history, team, locations, general facts. |
| 2 | Products / Services | `products/` | Product and service descriptions, features, capabilities, limitations, specifications. |
| 3 | Pricing / Commercial Terms | `pricing/` | Approved price lists, plans, discounts policy, payment and contract terms. |
| 4 | ICP / Target Customers | `icp/` | Ideal customer profiles, target segments, personas, qualification criteria. |
| 5 | Sales Playbooks | `sales_playbooks/` | Sales process, stages, qualification frameworks, recommended conversation flows. |
| 6 | FAQ | `faq/` | Approved answers to frequently asked customer questions. |
| 7 | Objections | `objections/` | Common objections and approved responses. |
| 8 | Case Studies | `case_studies/` | Approved customer stories, results and references cleared for external use. |
| 9 | Outbound Messaging | `outbound_messaging/` | Approved outreach templates, value propositions, tone and style guidance for cold/promotional email. |
| 10 | Industry Knowledge | `industry/` | Market context, industry terminology, trends and pain points relevant to prospects. |
| 11 | Competitor / Alternative Positioning | `competitors/` | Approved positioning versus competitors and alternatives; what may and may not be claimed. |
| 12 | Legal / Compliance / Approved Communication | `legal_compliance/` | Compliance rules, forbidden claims, required disclaimers, opt-out/unsubscribe requirements, approved wording. |
| 13 | Contact / Routing Knowledge | `contacts/` | Who handles what, escalation paths, routing rules for leads and requests. |
| 14 | Meeting / Next-Step Guidance | `meeting_guidance/` | How to propose meetings/demos, booking rules, recommended next steps per lead stage. |
| 15 | Approved Marketing Materials | `marketing_materials/` | Brochures, one-pagers, decks and links cleared for sharing with prospects. |
